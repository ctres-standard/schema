# Changelog

Versions follow §13 of the Standard: `MAJOR.MINOR` is the version of the Standard the schema implements, and `PATCH` corrects the schema without changing the record model. See [README.md](README.md#versioning).

## 1.1.0 — 4 November 2026

Implements CTRES v1.1: the record model of v1.0 extended for gaming revenue and the operator's levy, and the conformance levels of §10 defined by scope. Packages produced under 1.0 remain valid; they keep `standard_version` `1.0`.

### Added

* `schema/records/r13_gaming_revenue_statement.schema.json`: R13 Gaming Revenue Statement (§4.13), one statement per licence, product line, channel, currency and period, with stakes and winnings on a declared recognition basis, bonus, jackpot, network and open-liability components, the operator's GGR and its formula, the self-assessed levy where the profile's figure of record is the operator's declaration, and the authority's own figure where the authority computes one. A line of a venue carries `venue_id` and a line split by player location carries `player_location`, both conditional on the profile's aggregation axes; `stakes_basis` accepts `accrual_at_result`, as the profile's `recognition.stakes_basis` does; `stakes_bonus` or `winnings_bonus` requires `bonus_measurement`, `bonus_granted` and `bonus_expired`. A statement of kind `correction` requires `corrects_statement_id` and `correction_reason`. The properties are in the order of the field table of §4.13: the eighteen core fields every statement carries, from `statement_id` to `evidence_ref`, then the conditional and optional fields from `corrects_statement_id` to `authority_figure_ref`; the examples follow the same order.
* `schema/records/r14_venue_count.schema.json`: R14 Venue Count (§4.14), one row per venue, unit and gaming day or period. A `slot_machine` row requires meter readings at the start and the end unless `meter_source` is `not_recorded` (the GLI-11 §3.9.7 set: `coin_in` and `coin_out` mandatory; `jackpots_hand_paid`, `cancelled_credits`, `progressive_payouts_machine_paid`, `progressive_payouts_attendant_paid`, `bill_in`, `ticket_in`, `ticket_out`, `promo_in`, `promo_out`, `wat_in`, `wat_out` and `games_played` optional), and the drop and fills where it wins by `slot_drop_formula`; a `table` row requires inventories, fills, credits and drop; a `counter` row requires inventories; a `venue_total` row requires the count-room totals and the variance. `win_formula` fits the unit: a slot formula or `profile` on a `slot_machine` row, `table_drop_formula` or `profile` on a `table` row, any on `counter` and `venue_total` rows.
* `schema/profile.schema.json`: the optional `revenue_and_levy` block (§5 Revenue and levy): five parameters a validator reads and a notes block. `levy_formula` is one entry per product line (`applies_to`, with `*` for every line, and optionally a `jurisdiction_category`): `base_type` (`ggr`, `turnover`, `fixed_per_unit`, `commission`, `fee_band`, `prize_pool`), `terms` (the R13 decimal quantities that enter the base, each with `sign` `+` or `-` and an optional `cap` as a share of another quantity or as an amount), exactly one of `rate`, `bands` and `fixed_amount`, and `venue_win_formulas` with the values of R14 `win_formula`; `recognition` carries `stakes_basis` and `winnings_basis` with the R13 enumerations; `period_and_filing` carries `period` (`gaming_day`, `week`, `month`, `quarter`, `year`), `cut_off`, `due`, `collector` (`tax_authority`, `regulator`, `both`, `other`), `channel`, `form` and `figure_of_record` (`operator_declaration`, `authority_computation`); `negative_periods` carries `rule` (`carry_forward`, `floor_zero`, `none`) and `netting_scope` (`line`, `product_class`, `licence`); `aggregation` carries `axes` (`product_line`, `jurisdiction_category`, `channel`, `venue`, `player_location`, `currency`, `brand`) and a versioned `taxonomy` reference; `notes` is free text keyed `stake_scope`, `bonus_treatment`, `refunds_and_voids`, `chargebacks_and_bad_debts`, `jackpot_and_pool_contributions`, `taxes_in_base`, `evidence_linkage`, `land_based` and `source`. Bonus, refund, chargeback, jackpot and tax treatments are terms of the formula, not separate switches. Where present, the block carries `levy_formula`, `recognition` and `period_and_filing`, and every object in it is closed. `ctres_version` is `1.0` or `1.1`; a profile with the block declares `1.1`. Submission modes may list R13 and R14, and `submission.minimum_conformance_level` (`level_1` to `level_4`) sets the level the authority requires (§10).
* `schema/manifest.schema.json`: `standard_version` is `1.0` or `1.1`; `files[].record_type` accepts R13 and R14; a package that lists an R13 or R14 file declares `1.1`. `conformance_level_declared` accepts `level_4`, and its four values name the levels of §10, defined by scope: `level_1` Return (manifest + R13), `level_2` Evidence (+ R3, R4, R7, and R14 where an R13 line has channel `venue`), `level_3` Custody (+ R1, R2, R6, R10, R11), `level_4` Supervision (+ R5, R8, R9, R12).
* `schema/records/r01_funds_location.schema.json`: optional `venue_id`, so that an R14 row can be tied to the venue whose cash float the location is.
* `schema/common.schema.json`: `levy_return` in the report-type enumeration used by R12, the manifest's report-reference counts and profile report triggers.
* `examples/`: three R13 statements (an online casino line with `player_location`, a venue line with `venue_id`, and a correction), three R14 rows (slot machine, table, venue total), a 1.1 package manifest declaring `level_3`, and version 1.1.0 of the fictional XJ profile with a Revenue and levy block (a GGR base with bonus stakes in and bonus granted deductible up to 20% of cash stakes, a commission base for peer-to-peer products, a fixed amount per machine for venue machines; monthly; carried forward per product class; split by product line and channel) and a minimum conformance level of `level_3`.
* `tests/validate_examples.py`: expects 19 schemas; broken and varied copies for the new conditions, among them a levy formula term with an unknown quantity or a sign other than `+` and `-`, a block without `levy_formula`, a manifest declaring `level_4` (valid) and `level_5` (invalid).

### Changed

* Every `$id` moves from `https://ctres.org/schema/1.0/` to `https://ctres.org/schema/1.1/`; references between files stay relative. Schema titles say v1.1.
* The conformance levels are defined by scope (§10): a core (manifest, R3, R4, R7, R13) and the Custody (R1, R2, R6, R10, R11), Supervision (R5, R8, R9, R12) and Venue (R14) modules, added in four steps. The former `level_1` to `level_3` of v1.0 (Records, Structured, Continuous), which described delivery maturity, are replaced; delivery is the manifest `submission_mode` of §8.1 and is independent of the level. Field requirements inside a record do not change with the level; at `level_1` and `level_2` a reference to a record type the level does not require is an identifier in the operator's own systems, present in the record and not resolved within the package. In a package that declares `standard_version` `1.0`, `conformance_level_declared` keeps its v1.0 meaning.

## 1.0.1 — 3 November 2026

- Descriptions only: references to a pre-release draft removed. No change to fields, requirements or validation.

## 1.0.0 — 2026-10-05

Draft for consultation. First release, implementing CTRES v1.0, universal edition (2 October 2026).

### Added

* `schema/common.schema.json`: decimal strings, timestamps with an explicit offset, dates, durations, ISO 4217 codes and virtual-asset identifiers, identifiers, the player key pair, the `not_recorded` marker for visible gaps (§3), fees and tax lines, and shared enumerations.
* `schema/rail_modules.schema.json`: rail modules for card, bank transfer and instant payments, e-money, mobile money and carrier billing, vouchers, cash, and virtual assets (§6).
* `schema/payment.schema.json` and `schema/records/r01_funds_location.schema.json` to `r12_report_reference.schema.json`: the twelve record types of §4, with conditional requirements expressed as `if`/`then` where the record itself shows the condition.
* `schema/manifest.schema.json`: the package manifest of §8.2, including supersession of an earlier package and counts of report references where R12 is withheld (§4.12).
* `schema/profile.schema.json`: the structure of a jurisdiction profile (§5): versioning and effective dates, status, inheritance from a base profile, indexed units, and one parameter block for each row of the §5 table.
* `examples/`: the five Annex C examples, completed with the fields Annex C omits; synthetic examples of every record type; two manifests; and two profiles for the fictional jurisdiction XJ.
* `tests/validate_examples.py` and a GitHub Actions workflow that runs it.
